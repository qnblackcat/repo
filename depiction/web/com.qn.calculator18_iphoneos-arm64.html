Calculator18

Changelog

Features:

  • Full expression shown above the result.
  • iOS 18-style calculator key (bottom-left) with a menu to switch between basic and scientific keypads, or open a New Note. Combine it with NoteMath18 to open Apple Notes and perform complex calculations.
  • Clear key works like iOS 18: it deletes one character at a time, and holding it clears everything.
  • Fully support for landscape mode.
