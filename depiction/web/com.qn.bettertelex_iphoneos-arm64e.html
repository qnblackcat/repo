0.2.1

  • Experimental iOS 14 support (untested on real devices; falls back to stock keyboard behaviour if it can't read the composition state).
  • Rootless and rootful builds available alongside roothide.

0.2.0

  • Initial release.
  • Fixes the stock Vietnamese Telex keyboard changing words you already typed correctly when you go back to edit them (e.g. what no longer turns into ưhats, isp no longer becomes íp).
  • Vietnamese words still get their tone marks fixed as usual.
