0.0.1

  • Initial release, inspired by CleanCopy by b4db1r3.
  • Resolves short links (Google /goto?url=, Reddit /s/, amzn.to) to the real destination without opening the page.
  • Unwraps redirects (google.com/url?q=, Google Ads, Facebook l.php, LinkedIn).
  • Strips tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, si…) and share junk from Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X, Reddit, LinkedIn and Spotify.
  • Shortens Amazon links to /dp/<ASIN>.
  • Works system-wide, including links inside copied text. If resolving a short link fails or takes over 4 s, the original link is copied.
