1.0.2

  • Rewrote the code: Low Power Mode is now turned back on only when the system turns it off automatically, replacing the previous timing-based detection of manual turn-off.

1.0.1

  • Fixed SpringBoard crashing when PleaseKeepLPM turned Low Power Mode back on.

1.0.0

  • Initial release.
  • iOS turns Low Power Mode off once the battery charges past 80%; PleaseKeepLPM turns it back on automatically while the device is plugged in.
  • Turning Low Power Mode off yourself (Control Center or Settings) is respected.
