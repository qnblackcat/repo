Changelog

Two-line clock in the iOS status bar: the time on top, the date below.

  • Pick a preset format for each line, or type your own.
  • Seconds that tick, and an optional blinking colon.
  • Font size, bold and italic per line, plus a vertical offset.
  • Always hides the location indicator, and optionally the Focus icon (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, Work…) and the breadcrumb back-link.
  • Experimental: two lines in the pill that replaces the clock (screen recording, calls, hotspot).
  • Made for Face ID iPhones, including the Dynamic Island. Does NOT work on Touch ID devices.
