1.9.4

  • The unlock toast now also shows "PassBy: Magic passcode" every time you unlock with the magic passcode (setting renamed to "Show unlock toast").
  • Magic passcode unlocks are confirmed against the real lock state, since the unlock can finish asynchronously.

1.9.3

  • New (Experimental): guest passcode for lending your phone. It unlocks normally, re-locks after a set timeout (0 = never) and keeps auto-unlock off until you unlock with Face ID/Touch ID or your real passcode.
  • The guest passcode must be numeric (4 or 6 digits) and the same length as your real passcode.
  • Updated help page.

1.9.2

  • iOS 15+ with rootless and roothide support.
  • New: optional toast when the device is unlocked by a trusted WiFi network or Bluetooth device (with a test button in settings).
  • Removed Activator integration and the CoverSheet auto-dismiss option.
  • Bug fixes and cleanup.
