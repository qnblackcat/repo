Tapbacks18

Changelog

  • An attempt to bring the Tapbacks feature from iOS 18+ down to iOS 16 / 17.

Features

  • A round "+" button beside the six classic tapbacks opens the emoji keyboard; the picked emoji is sent as a tapback.
  • If your own tapback is an emoji, the "+" button shows it (blue); tap it to change it, or tap the ✕ badge to remove it. This also works when you open the picker by tapping an existing tapback on a message.
  • Emoji tapbacks from other people are drawn in the tapback platter.
  • Between devices that all run Tapbacks18, emoji land on the exact message, including photos and files.
  • iOS 18+ devices without the tweak: their emoji tapbacks arrive as plain text ("Reacted 🔥 to “…”", 45 languages) and are turned back into tapbacks. Text messages match by content; photos/files by "newest message with an attachment" unless the native setting is on (see below).

Native emoji tapbacks

iOS 18+ sends the native emoji tapback (amt 2006/3006 + ame + amk = exact target) only to devices whose IDS registration advertises supports-stick-moji-backs; others get a plain-text fallback with no target GUID for photos, videos and attachments.

  • Turn it on only if you want emoji from iOS 18+ devices without Tapbacks18 to show on the exact photo or file. You do not need it for text messages, or between devices that all have Tapbacks18.
  • Tapbacks18IDS (identityservicesd) adds the capability to the iMessage client data. It applies only after iMessage registers again: toggle iMessage off and on (Settings → Messages → iMessage) after turning it on or off. This changes the registration data sent to Apple. It has been tested on the developer's own devices without any sign of being flagged, but use it at your own risk.
  • Before uninstalling Tapbacks18, turn it off and toggle iMessage again; otherwise iOS 18+ keeps sending emoji tapbacks that this device cannot show.

Known limitations

  • Notifications and the conversation-list preview say "loved …" for an emoji tapback. Those strings are built from the classic tapback type (IMTapback previewString…), which only sees the heart. Not fixed.
  • Sticker tapbacks from iOS 18 (amt 2007/3007) become a plain-text message ("Reacted with a sticker").
  • If the tweak is not loaded (e.g. reboot into jailed mode), emoji tapbacks show as hearts.
  • iOS 16/17 devices without the tweak may not display amt 2006 at all.
  • The pinned-conversation tapback bubble (CKPinnedConversationTapbackBubble) does not show emoji.
  • Tapbacks stored by very old builds that carried a tbr summary key are no longer handled.
