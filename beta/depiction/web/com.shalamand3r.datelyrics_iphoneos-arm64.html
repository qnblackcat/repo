3.0.7

  • Fixed Music crashing on iOS 16 when lyric timings or the playback position were invalid (NaN), e.g. while the queue reloads.

3.0.6

  • Fixed a paused lyric reappearing on the Lock Screen after its pause timeout, for example when another app takes over playback.
  • Smooth Word Sweep: the setting now notes its extra battery and GPU cost.

3.0.5

  • New (Experimental): Smooth Word Sweep lights each syllable gradually over its sung duration, like Apple Music. Holds whole syllables while the screen is off (Always-On).
  • New: Italic Lyrics option under Typography.

3.0.2

  • Roothide support.
  • New: full-screen artwork mode, instrumental dots and idle ticker.
