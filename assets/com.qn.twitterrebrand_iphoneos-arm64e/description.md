A jailbreak tweak that makes the X app look like Twitter again, without patching the IPA, based on [neofreebird](https://github.com/NeoFreeBird/app).

Changing the icon needs [my fork of NeoFreeBird](https://github.com/qnblackcat/neofreebird-tweak); the upstream [NeoFreeBird](https://github.com/NeoFreeBird/app) does not have this feature.
