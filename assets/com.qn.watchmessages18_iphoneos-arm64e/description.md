Keeps the Messages app on Apple Watches running watchOS 11.4 or newer when the paired iPhone is on iOS 15 – 18.3.

watchOS 11.4 treats Messages as a removable system app that the watch keeps only while the iPhone reports its Messages app as available. iOS 18.4+ does that; older iOS does not, so the watch loses Messages. WatchMessages18 makes older iOS report it, so Messages stays on the watch. No re-pairing needed.

## Notes
- This does not make an iPhone pair with a newer watchOS; pairing is a separate problem.
- If Messages is already gone from the watch, reinstall it from the App Store on the watch after installing the tweak.
